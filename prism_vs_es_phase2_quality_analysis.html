Query 10 — D7 sell-through rate by recommendation bucket (iOS)
D7 STR across all 5 categories — 4 recommendation buckets
Bucket 1 — Prism lower + Adopted Bucket 2 — Prism same/higher + Adopted Bucket 3 — Prism lower + Not Adopted Bucket 4 — Prism same/higher + Not Adopted
Adoption drives sell-through. Adopted listings (Buckets 1 & 2) consistently outperform non-adopted listings (Bucket 3) across all 5 categories. Bucket 2 (Prism same/higher + Adopted) leads overall at 15.6% D7 STR — sellers who follow Prism's guidance sell fastest. Bucket 3 (Prism lower, not adopted) is the weakest at 6.1%, suggesting sellers who override a lower-priced suggestion are leaving conversion on the table. Bucket 3 is also the largest volume bucket at 222K listings — the clearest signal that Prism is calibrating lower than the market for a significant share of listings.

Prism does not suppress listing volume. Established sellers are effectively flat (−0.4%) — no friction for experienced listers. The standout signal is New Listers +6.8% — sellers who listed for the first time in the last 90 days post nearly one additional listing per lister with Prism. This is a strong indication that price guidance reduces friction at the most critical moment: a seller's first-ever listings. Non-Seller Activated users also post +2.8% more listings on average, consistent with Prism lowering the barrier for uncommitted sellers. Segment mix is near-identical between groups (~84% established, ~8% new listers, ~8% non-activated), ruling out composition bias.

Established Seller web −10.0pp is the standout concern. New Listers and Non-SA are strongly positive on web (up to +15.2pp), so this is isolated to experienced sellers — likely a web serving or display issue, not a model quality problem.
On mobile, when Prism serves a range, new listers complete at +2.5–4.2pp higher rates and non-activated sellers at +2.6–3.0pp higher rates vs ES. The web signal for new listers is even stronger at +15.2pp — though on a smaller covered base. Prism's guidance is most effective for beginner sellers who have no prior pricing anchor — exactly the segment that benefits most from recommendations.
